Privacy Policy

Learn With Fun

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Overview

Learn With Fun ("we", "our", or "us") is an educational app for children. We are committed to protecting the privacy of the children who use it. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it is used, and the choices available to parents.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or sell any personal information. We do not have our own servers, and the app has no accounts or logins.

The app keeps the following only on the user's device:

  • The first name or nickname the child types on the welcome screen, used only to greet them in the app
  • Game progress, scores, stars and stickers
  • App settings and preferences

This information never leaves the device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads to support its development. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear on menu and results screens and are not shown while a child is answering a question. Children can also choose to watch a short video ad to get a hint; this is always optional and the child can say "No thanks".

Because this app is directed at children, we have configured AdMob as follows:

  • Every ad request is tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and for users under the age of consent
  • Only non-personalized ads are requested — no interest-based advertising or remarketing
  • Ad content is limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating
  • The app does not access the device's Advertising ID (the Advertising ID permission is blocked)

To deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown, and prevent fraud, Google may automatically receive limited technical information such as the device's IP address, device model, operating system, app version and ad interaction information. This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and child-directed treatment

COPPA and Children's Privacy

This app is designed for children and is built to follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not:

  • Collect personal information from children
  • Share children's information with third parties for their own marketing
  • Allow children to make their information publicly available
  • Show personalized or interest-based ads
  • Include social features, chat, or links to social media

Permissions

The app uses the following permissions:

  • Internet / network state: Used only to load ads from Google AdMob. All learning content works offline.
  • Vibrate: Used for haptic feedback during gameplay

The app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob (described above). The app does not use analytics or tracking tools and has no social media integration.

Data Security and Retention

Since we do not collect personal information, we do not hold any user data. Information saved by the app stays on the device, protected by the device's built-in security, until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

For Parents

We encourage parents to supervise their children's use of apps and to talk with them about online safety. You can remove all information the app has saved by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. If you have any questions or requests about your child's privacy, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

International Users

The app can be used worldwide. We do not collect or transfer personal information. Information that Google receives to serve ads is handled under Google's policies linked above.
